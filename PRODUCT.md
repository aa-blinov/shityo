# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web application. Desktop, laptop and tablet are the primary work surfaces;
phone layouts provide a compact drawing area and overlay panels.

## Users

Independent tailors and beginning pattern designers working from individual
measurements. A typical job is preparing a client's block for a printed toile.

## Product Purpose

Turn body measurements into editable garment pieces and full-scale printable
patterns. Success means the drawing, seam measurements and exported PDF agree.

## Positioning

Four deterministic drafting blocks share one 2D editor, approximate 3D preview
and paper-layout engine. External pattern browsing preserves publisher identity.
No exclusive market claim or validated fit guarantee has been established.

## Operating Context

Choose a local block, enter measurements, adjust style or supported construction
points, compare seams, save a design, then print at 100 percent and measure the
calibration square. External models open on the publisher's website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Tee, shirt, trouser and skirt blocks; local projects with JSON transfer; SVG,
DXF and tiled PDF; 85 external source entries. Storage is local to the browser.
3D does not simulate industrial fit. Arbitrary freehand pattern authoring,
external-pattern editing, accounts, cloud sync and production grading are absent.
Top blocks currently estimate the waist-to-hip distance as 20 cm.

## Brand Commitments

Shityo is the product name. Monos UI supplies the component vocabulary, six
palettes and light/dark modes. Russian and English are supported.

## Evidence on Hand

The drafting engine and tests live in backend. Browser workflows cover search,
project validation and transfer, printing and responsive layouts. Source records
carry publisher addresses, attribution and verification dates. The screenshots
use demonstration measurements. No customer counts or fit trials are claimed.

## Product Principles

1. Keep dimensions and exports consistent, with explicit units.
2. Preserve user work and reject invalid imported state before applying it.
3. Attribute real designs and distinguish the external catalog from local blocks.
4. Give the drawing space before adding controls or decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Controls use labels and visible focus. Construction coordinates have numeric
inputs as an alternative to dragging. Line meanings have text labels. Responsive
checks are browser emulation, not physical-device or formal WCAG certification.
